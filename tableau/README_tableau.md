\# 📊 Heart Failure Dashboard (Tableau)



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project presents an interactive \*\*Tableau dashboard\*\* analyzing heart failure clinical data. The goal is to visually explore patient characteristics and identify key factors influencing mortality using intuitive and user-friendly dashboards.



The dashboards are designed to support data-driven insights in healthcare by highlighting trends, patterns, and risk factors associated with heart failure.



---



\## ⚙️ Tool Used



\* \*\*Tableau\*\* – Data visualization and dashboard creation



---



\## 📂 Dataset Description



The dataset contains clinical information of heart failure patients, including:



\* Age

\* Sex

\* Smoking status

\* Anaemia

\* Diabetes

\* High blood pressure

\* Serum creatinine

\* Death event (target variable)



---



\## 📊 Dashboard Overview



\### 1️⃣ Demographic Overview



\* Displays distribution of patients by age, sex, smoking habits, and high blood pressure

\* Provides an understanding of the overall patient population



---



\### 2️⃣ Mortality Analysis



\* Shows distribution of death events (survived vs deceased)

\* Highlights imbalance between outcomes



---



\### 3️⃣ Age vs Mortality Risk



\* Illustrates how age impacts the likelihood of death

\* Identifies high-risk age groups



---



\### 4️⃣ Clinical Factors Impact



\* Compares key medical conditions:



&nbsp; \* Diabetes

&nbsp; \* Anaemia

&nbsp; \* High blood pressure

\* Demonstrates their influence on patient mortality



---



\## 📈 Key Insights



\* Patients aged above 60 show a higher risk of death

\* High blood pressure and diabetes are strongly associated with mortality

\* Anaemia contributes to increased health risk

\* Majority of patients survived, indicating class imbalance



---



\## 🚀 How to Use



1\. Open the `.twbx` file in Tableau

2\. Navigate between dashboards

3\. Use filters and interactive elements to explore data



---



\## 🎯 Objective



To demonstrate the use of \*\*Tableau dashboards\*\* in analyzing healthcare data and extracting meaningful insights related to heart failure and patient outcomes.



---



\## 📌 Future Improvements



\* Add advanced filters and parameters

\* Include predictive insights using integrated models

\* Enhance dashboard interactivity and design
